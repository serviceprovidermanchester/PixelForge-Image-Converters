## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- **Monorepo Suite Launch:** Released full enterprise collection of 60 standalone, zero-upload client-side image converters.
- **Corporate Branding Integration:** Full branding under **SPManchester Private Limited** and authored by **Sayed Mudassar Akbar**.
- **Central Portal Hub (`index.html`):** Real-time client-side search, format category filters, responsive dark glassmorphism design.
- **Supported Formats:** Bidirectional conversions across PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, BMP, GIF, ICO, AVIF, PDF, and Base64 Data URLs.
- **Batch Processing:** Multi-file drag-and-drop processing with client-side uncompressed ZIP packaging in browser memory.
- **Enterprise DevOps:** Added production-ready `Dockerfile`, `docker-compose.yml`, and hardened `nginx.conf` with security headers.
- **GitHub Governance:** Comprehensive `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `SUPPORT.md`, issue templates, and automated CI/CD workflows.
- **Official Links:** Integrated corporate website [www.spmanchester.com](https://www.spmanchester.com) and enterprise contact [info@spmanchester.com](mailto:info@spmanchester.com).
